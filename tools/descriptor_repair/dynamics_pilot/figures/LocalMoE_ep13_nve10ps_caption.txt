Figure X. NVE energy stability of Local-MoE for 52 SiO2 interface structures (30 Oishi and 22 constructed interfaces), containing 162-4608 atoms. (a) Change in total energy per atom relative to the first NVE sample. The shaded band shows the minimum and maximum across all structures and samples in 0.04 ps time bins; the line is the equal-weight mean of the per-structure bin means. (b) Maximum absolute energy excursion and (c) ordinary-least-squares linear drift rate, both calculated over the same 0-10 ps interval. 52 of 52 structures satisfy the chosen maximum-excursion criterion of 0.1 meV/atom; the largest excursion is 0.008297 meV/atom. Following structural relaxation and 5 ps NPT equilibration at 300 K and 1 bar, velocities were rescaled to an initial NVE temperature of 300 K. The reported NVE protocol used ASE VelocityVerlet with a 0.5 fs timestep and fixed cell volume.
