Figure X. NVE energy stability of Local-MoE for 10 SiO2 interface structures (5 Oishi and 5 constructed interfaces), containing 216-4608 atoms. (a) Change in total energy per atom relative to the first NVE sample. The shaded band shows the minimum and maximum across all structures and samples in 2 ps time bins; the line is the equal-weight mean of the per-structure bin means. (b) Maximum absolute energy excursion and (c) ordinary-least-squares linear drift rate, both calculated over the same 0-500 ps interval. 9 of 10 structures satisfy the chosen maximum-excursion criterion of 0.1 meV/atom; the largest excursion is 0.519446 meV/atom.
